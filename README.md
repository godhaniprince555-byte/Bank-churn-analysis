Bank Churn Analysis
Objective: To analyze customer behavior and identify the primary factors driving bank customer churn.

Approach:

Source Data Review: Conducted an initial assessment of the raw dataset to understand its structure.

Data Preparation: Cleaned and transformed the data utilizing Power Query Editor.

Data Modeling & Analysis: Structured the data model to enable accurate exploratory data analysis.

DAX Measures: Formulated custom Data Analysis Expressions (DAX) to calculate key performance indicators (KPIs).

Dashboard Development: Designed and built an interactive Power BI dashboard for data visualization.

Key Insights:

Overall Churn: Out of 10,000 total customers, 2,037 have churned, resulting in a baseline churn rate of 20.4%.

Demographics: Male customers account for the majority of the churned population at 54.57%. Additionally, the churn rate peaks dramatically at 56.2% for customers in the 51-60 age group.

Credit Status: Customers with a credit score of 400 or below exhibit a significantly higher likelihood of leaving the bank.

Product Ownership: Customers holding 'Product 1' experience the highest churn rate across the board at 69.71%.

Recommendations:

Targeted Senior Products: Develop specialized banking products aimed at senior customers nearing retirement to improve retention in this high-risk demographic.

Loyalty Incentives: Implement a reward or incentive program for long-tenured customers to foster continued loyalty and account longevity.

High-Value Account Packages: Introduce exclusive benefits—such as travel and leisure packages or subsidized investment portfolios—specifically for customers with account balances exceeding 200k.
